# The Odd Department Autopilot

The Odd Department makes short videos about the strange side of science, history and everyday life. *The world is stranger than it looks.*

The Odd Department Autopilot is a small private tool used only by the owner of The Odd Department channel. It reads our own channel's statistics (views, watch time, likes and comments) so we can learn which videos people enjoy and make better ones.

It is not a public service and it does not access anyone else's account.
